The purpose of this analysis was originally to fins out what final
performance metrics predict wins more than others, what match stats move
the needle the most. I understand the underlying mechanisms of
generating those stats are much more iluminating for the purpose of
acquiring wins, but for that kind of analysis in-match event logs would
be required, which unfortunately is not available in Halo Infinite (not
at scale any way, I've seen projects to generate heatmap data for a
single match, but scaling this to gather a large sample is outside my
competence, for now at least). This analysis gathers finals match data
from Ranked Arena matchmaking, analysis of HCS matches is done in a
seprate notebook.

For analysis, I have focused on team performance, for two reasons.
Firstly, the matchmaking stats are retrieved via the Halo Infinite API,
which technically accesses the match record via a single
player/gamertag, that is it retrieves the numbers for gamertag
``accessing'' the API, so for a single retriaval no stats for other
individual players are available. The workaround would be to access the
match\_id records from ``perspective'' of each player in the lobby and
then join individual performances, this will likely be done in the
future, since I wish to analyse how much good or poor performance of
team mates affects individual stats. Alas, for now the data required is
not available. Secondly, analysing strictly at the aggregate team level
is sufficient if we assume that individual peformances are strictly
upstream, that is there are no edges from individual performance stats
to win-nerating processes downstream from team performance.
